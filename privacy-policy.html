Our Commitment to Your Privacy

At UMNNI Advertising, we respect your privacy and recognise the importance of handling personal information responsibly. This policy explains how we handle information submitted through our auto rickshaw advertising website.

01 Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the UMNNI Advertising auto rickshaw advertising landing page and enquiries submitted through this website.

UMNNI Advertising offers outdoor and transit advertising solutions, including rear panel advertising, side branding, hood branding, full auto wraps and fleet advertising campaigns.

02 Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry or request a consultation, we collect the information you provide, which may include:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Mobile number
  • Preferred advertising format
  • Campaign requirements and messages
  • City, vehicle quantity and campaign duration if included in your message
  • The website form through which your enquiry was submitted

Our hosting and external resource providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details, device information and access times.

Our enquiry form does not request payment information, banking credentials or government identification numbers. Please avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive information.

03 How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through this website may be used to:

  • Respond to auto advertising enquiries.
  • Contact you by phone or email regarding your request.
  • Understand your preferred locations and campaign objectives.
  • Prepare advertising proposals and quotations.
  • Coordinate requested advertising campaigns.
  • Maintain relevant business communication records.
  • Maintain website functionality and security.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

Separate permission will be sought for unrelated promotional communications where required.

04 Information Sharing

Enquiries submitted through our website are forwarded to our designated business email account so our team can respond to them.

Where necessary, information may be processed or shared with:

  • Relevant UMNNI Advertising team members.
  • Website hosting and email service providers.
  • Advertising partners required to coordinate requested services.
  • Authorised authorities where disclosure is required by law.

05 Cookies and Website Data

Cookies are small files that may be stored on your device by websites or external services.

Our website uses external font resources. External providers may process technical information according to their own privacy practices.

You can manage cookies and external resource permissions using your browser settings. Restricting certain resources may affect website appearance or functionality.

06 Data Security

We recognise the importance of protecting personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or misuse.

Our website uses server-side form processing and validation. Submitted enquiries are forwarded through an authenticated email service.

However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

07 Data Retention

Personal information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, coordinate requested campaigns and maintain relevant business records.

Information may also be retained where required for contractual, accounting or applicable legal obligations.

08 Third-Party Services

Our website uses third-party services supporting website functionality and business communication.

  • Google Fonts for website typography.
  • Google email services for receiving enquiries.
  • Website hosting and infrastructure services.
  • External website links.

These services operate according to their own terms and privacy policies.

Google's privacy practices can be reviewed at: Google Privacy Policy .

09 Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may contact us to request:

  • Access to your personal information.
  • Correction of inaccurate information.
  • Deletion of personal information.
  • Withdrawal of consent where applicable.
  • Stopping optional promotional communications.
  • Review of privacy-related concerns.

Requests may be submitted by email to info@umnni.com . Identity verification may be required before certain requests are processed.

10 Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for businesses, organisations and individuals seeking professional advertising services.

It is not directed towards children under the age of 18. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through our website, please contact us.

11 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our website, services, business practices or applicable legal requirements.

Updated versions will be published on this page. Additional notice will be provided where required.
