Our Commitment to Your Privacy

UMNNI Advertising respects your privacy and aims to handle your personal information responsibly. This policy describes our auto rickshaw advertising landing page’s enquiry process.

01 Introduction

This policy applies to the UMNNI Advertising auto rickshaw advertising landing page and enquiries submitted through it. We offer auto rickshaw branding, rear and side panel advertising, hood branding and fleet campaigns.

02 Information We Collect

We collect the name, email address, phone number, selected service, message and form source provided when you submit an enquiry. Hosting and external resource providers may also process IP addresses, browser information and access logs. Please do not include sensitive information or payment credentials in your message.

03 How We Use Information

We use enquiry information to respond to consultation requests, understand business goals, recommend services, prepare proposals, coordinate requested work and maintain relevant business communications. We may contact you by phone or email regarding your enquiry. Unrelated promotional communication should be subject to separate permission where required.

04 Information Sharing

Enquiries are forwarded to our designated business email account. Information may be accessed by relevant team members, hosting and email providers, and service partners as necessary to respond to requests or fulfil services. We may disclose information where legally required.

05 Cookies and Website Data

This page loads Tailwind CSS from an external CDN, Google Fonts and an embedded Google Maps location. These providers may process technical information or use cookies according to their own policies. You can manage cookies in your browser; restricting external resources may affect website functionality.

06 Data Security

Our enquiry forms use server-side validation and authenticated SMTP email processing. We aim to protect information using appropriate safeguards; no internet transmission or storage method is completely secure.

07 Data Retention

Enquiry information may be retained as reasonably necessary to respond to requests, manage business relationships and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. Contact us to request deletion, subject to applicable requirements.

08 Third-Party Services

The website uses external styling resources, Google Fonts, Google Maps and Google email services. It also links to Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube and the main UMNNI website. These services have their own privacy practices. See Google Privacy Policy. Advertising services described on this website do not necessarily mean that advertising tracking is installed on this landing page.

09 Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction or deletion of your personal information, withdraw consent where relevant, opt out of optional promotional communication or raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

10 Children’s Privacy

This website is intended for individuals and organisations seeking professional advertising services and is not directed towards children. If you believe a child has submitted information, contact us so we can review the matter.

11 Policy Updates

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the website or our data handling practices. Updated versions will be published on this page.
